# Interpretation limits recorded during development

- Families, implementations and benchmark specifications share an author. Separate equations,
  adversarial probes and scripted traces reduce correlated errors; they do not replace an
  independent external evaluation.
- The three paired seed blocks also vary endowment or other case settings. Their bootstrap
  intervals describe this fixed heterogeneous diagnostic set, not a population of seeds under
  one fixed protocol. Repetitions are averaged within block, never treated as new environments.
- Larger teams trade rounds for agents under a common decision allowance. This is a resource
  configuration comparison, not an isolated causal estimate of team size.
- Numerical/citation/caveat success does not prove useful interpretation. The failed payoff
  calibration after an otherwise perfect numerical retest demonstrates this gap.
- Seven explicit rule-based worlds support up to twelve agents. They are controlled synthetic
  mechanisms, not calibrated social/ecological/physical replicas or arbitrarily extensible
  autonomous worlds. Retained experience changes context; it does not train model weights.
- Fresh model calls are stochastic. Exact replay reproduces saved actions and deterministic
  settlement, not the provider's future sample. Token-price estimates are not invoices;
  unknown requests retain conservative reservations.
- The kernel's strategy-discrimination check distinguishes scripted action traces. Analytical
  outcome checks and scoring mutation tests are separate evidence. Distinct strategies can
  produce tied payoffs; passing the kernel is not a statistical strategy-classifier score.
