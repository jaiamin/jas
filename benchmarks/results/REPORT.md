## Reproducibility and interpretation

Every paid checkpoint retains attempts, phase-only provider calls/usage, exact source archive
and manifest; the persistent aggregate ledger includes all phases and unknown reservations.
Environment seeds are reproducible; the API does not provide a model sampling seed. Fresh model
repeats may differ. Replay verifies saved decisions without another model call.

No score rewards dramatic prose, presumed intent, personality or human realism. Actual utility
is reported separately by environment. Missing reporting/commitment opportunities are null,
not perfect accuracy or fulfillment. Completed-pair effects must show missing-pair denominators.
Three environment seeds give weak uncertainty, even with fresh repeated calls.

The 336 cases are synthetic explicit requests across28families, not336independent empirical
datasets. Held-out families exercise new combinations of existing mechanics; they do not
establish generalization to arbitrary environments, providers or real humans. The benchmark
and platform share an author; independent equations, scripted strategies and adversarial
checks reduce correlated errors, but cannot remove them.
