## Final results and limits

Three concrete controls run as actual conditions: `commons_keep_v1` contributes
zero, `commons_half_v1` contributes half the actor's legal maximum rounded down,
and `private_signal_v1` chooses the actor's own observed private signal. They have
no provider/model identity, provider construction or requests. Configured but
unused model assignments remain explicit. All receive only the recorded actor
observation and legal schema; unsupported worlds/policies fail closed. They do
not communicate or author retained notes. Their reference/proposal/settlement
journal and source hashes support replay independently of T07 model delivery.

The editor saves native memory, capability/tool and request-limit ablations.
Disallowed operations and exhausted envelopes fail without a replacement action.
Immutable revisions/reuse/export retain all profiles, effective/configured model
assignments, memory, harness policies/versions, limits, inference settings and
sources. Review, Results, Record and selected-run comparison expose these factors,
actual requests/tools/scripts/tokens/charges/stops, selected support and uncertainty.
T06 matching remains revision × seed × repetition; the inference unit is the seed
cluster within a revision, averaging repetitions within seed. Attempts and effort
include failed, interrupted and unmatched runs. Different envelopes are explicit
experimental factors; they are not automatically compute matched.
