title: JAS
description: Controlled, replayable experiments on AI agents.
---

JAS is a local workspace for testing how AI agents behave under explicit rules.
Compare conditions with the same seeds, inspect what each agent saw, and keep a
record of its actions and their consequences.

## Start with an example

[Run JAS locally](/quickstart), then open **Learn → Examples**. Watch a bundled
recording without a provider key or model calls. Choose **Use example** to edit
its protocol; this does not start a run.

Recordings use declared scripted policies. They demonstrate the environment and
viewer, not model behavior.

## Ask, compare, inspect

1. **Define a question.** Choose an environment, participants, conditions and
   outcome. Review what changes and what stays matched.
2. **Run a bounded comparison.** Choose your models and spending limits, then
   explicitly start the work. Saving a protocol never runs it.
3. **Inspect the evidence.** Follow observations, actions and consequences, compare
   matched runs, and replay recorded rules without calling a model.

The [Assistant](/guides/experiments#work-with-the-assistant) can help draft a
protocol or interpret selected evidence. You review its proposals before saving
or running them.

## Keep your workspace local

Experiments, runs, conversations and provider keys stay on your machine. Local
use needs no account. [Publishing](/guides/publishing) sends an explicitly reviewed
record to a separate gallery.

JAS measures model behavior under a protocol. Results do not establish human
validity, intent, or how a model will behave outside those conditions.
