# Preserve evidence during publication review

Published records are immutable evidence, so silently redacting an observation
while keeping its checkpoint/hash or verification badge would misrepresent the
record. The user accepted editing and rescanning mutable publication text, with
documented dismissal of demonstrable scanner false positives; genuine credentials,
forbidden local paths and hidden reasoning cannot be waived. A real leak in frozen
evidence blocks that record, preserving its local original and requiring an
explicit clean version/run before publication.

Unlisted is the publication default and means readable by anyone with the link,
without discovery indexing. Unpublishing or takedown stops JAS serving the page,
record files and social card, removes listings and purges controlled caches while
retaining a minimal identity/lineage tombstone. Downloaded copies and external
caches cannot be recalled; immutable content does not imply irrevocable serving.
