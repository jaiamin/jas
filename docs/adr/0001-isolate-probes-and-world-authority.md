# Isolate researcher probes and preserve world authority

The user requires researcher probes to operate on isolated world copies, while
world rules independently enforce permissions, visibility and consequences.
Generated actions, explanations and analyses are untrusted proposals or
interpretations; they do not establish authoritative facts. A declared,
versioned adjudication mechanism may accept a fallible model judgment as an
input, retaining provenance and existing replay guarantees, but cannot
contradict executable facts or invent a participant's response. This preserves
rich interpretive environments without giving the researcher or a fluent
explanation authority to rewrite the experiment. State-copy isolation and
isolation of generated executable code are separate requirements.
